My project work spans agri-food research, sustainability, regional development, and the promotion of agricultural products. The roles below follow my curriculum vitae: researcher in the first group and scientific associate in the second.

## Research Projects

- **2025–2026 — Regional Support Mechanism for Climate Change Adaptation in Crete.** Hellenic Mediterranean University. Funded by the Green Fund through an agreement with the Region of Crete.

- **2025 — SOILCRATES: Soil Innovation Labs — Co-Regenerating and Transforming European Soil.** Athens University of Economics and Business. Horizon Europe.

- **2025 — Organisational Restructuring in the Public Sector (DOPDA).** Research on change management and quality leadership at the Hellenic Open University.

- **2024–2025 — JustReDI: Resilience, Inclusion and Development.** Research on a just green and digital transition in Greek regions at Athena Research Center. Funded through Greece's National Recovery and Resilience Plan.

- **2021–2022 — Food Waste Awareness Campaign (FWAC).** Research and public awareness activities to reduce food waste in Greek households. University of Patras; funded by the Green Fund.

- **2019 — GREENUS.** Erasmus+ project on waste management and recycling with partners in South-East Asia. Sapienza University of Rome.

## Regional Development and Agri-Food Projects

I participated in the following projects as a scientific associate working with the Region of Western Greece and its agri-food partners.

- **2023–2024 — AgriFINoTRegion: Strengthening Financial Capacity for Smart Agrifood Regions.** Horizon 2020 project.
- **2023–2024 — GelatON the Road.** Creative Europe project.
- **2023–2024 — OLEAS.** Project supported by the Greek Rural Development Programme 2014–2020.
- **2023–2024 — Ancient Olenia.** Project supported by the Greek Rural Development Programme 2014–2020.
- **2023–2024 — MONOPATI.** Project supported by the Greek Rural Development Programme 2014–2020.
- **2023–2024 — Mediterranean Aperitivo: Enjoy European Quality!** European agri-food promotion project.
- **2023 — Western Greece Seafood.** Promotion of regional seafood in domestic and international markets.
- **2023 — Pollinators and Beekeeping.** Awareness activities concerning pollinators, beekeeping, and the preservation of pollinator ecosystems.
- **2022 — Wines of Western Greece.** Promotion of protected-designation wines in South Korea and Japan.
