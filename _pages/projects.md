My project work spans agri-food research, sustainability, regional development, and the promotion of agricultural products. The roles below follow my curriculum vitae: researcher in the first group and scientific associate in the second.

## Research Projects

- **2025–2026 — [Regional Support Mechanism for Climate Change Adaptation in Crete](https://www.crete.gov.gr/ekkinisi-tis-klimatikis-symmachias-tis-kritis-me-amesi-ylopoiisi-trion-ergon-gia-tin-prosarmogi-stin-klimatiki-allagi/).** Hellenic Mediterranean University. Funded by the Green Fund through an agreement with the Region of Crete.

- **2025 — [SOILCRATES: Soil Innovation Labs — Co-Regenerating and Transforming European Soil](https://soilcrates.eu/).** Athens University of Economics and Business. Horizon Europe.

- **2025 — Organisational Restructuring in the Public Sector (DOPDA).** Research on change management and quality leadership at the Hellenic Open University.

- **2024–2025 — [JustReDI: Resilience, Inclusion and Development](https://project.justredi.gr/en/home-en/).** Research on a just green and digital transition in Greek regions at Athena Research Center. Funded through Greece's National Recovery and Resilience Plan.

- **2021–2022 — [Food Waste Awareness Campaign (FWAC)](https://www.boroume.gr/enimerosi/ta-nea-mas/imerida-tis-summahias-gia-ti-meiosi-tis-spatalis-trofimon/).** Research and public awareness activities to reduce food waste in Greek households. University of Patras; funded by the Green Fund.

- **2019 — [GREENUS](https://greenus-project.eu/).** Erasmus+ project on waste management and recycling with South-East Asian partners. Sapienza University of Rome.

## Regional Development and Agri-Food Projects

I participated in the following projects as a scientific associate working with the Region of Western Greece and its agri-food partners.

- **2023–2024 — [AgriFINoTRegion: Strengthening Financial Capacity for Smart Agrifood Regions](https://drural.eu/news/drurals-mirror-region-agrifinotregion-at-agrown-2023/).** Horizon 2020 project.
- **2023–2024 — [GelatON the Road](https://www.agrifoodwest.gr/gelaton-the-road/).** Creative Europe project.
- **2023–2024 — [OLEAS](https://www.agrifoodwest.gr/metro-16-oleas/).** Project supported by the Greek Rural Development Programme 2014–2020.
- **2023–2024 — [Ancient Olenia](https://arxaia-oleneia.agrifoodwest.gr/).** Project supported by the Greek Rural Development Programme 2014–2020.
- **2023–2024 — [MONOPATI](https://monopati.agrifoodwest.gr/).** Project supported by the Greek Rural Development Programme 2014–2020.
- **2023–2024 — [Mediterranean Aperitivo: Enjoy European Quality!](https://enjoy-its-from-europe.campaign.europa.eu/en/campaigns/project/map_2022).** European agri-food promotion project.
- **2023 — [Western Greece Seafood](https://www.epimetol.gr/en/category/proothisi-provoli-alieumatwn/).** Promotion of regional seafood in domestic and international markets.
- **2023 — [Pollinators and Beekeeping](https://www.upatras.gr/ekdilosi-ston-votaniko-kipo-17-05-2023/).** Awareness activities concerning pollinators, beekeeping, and the preservation of pollinator ecosystems.
- **2022 — [Wines of Western Greece](https://winesofwesterngreece.com/).** Promotion of protected-designation wines in South Korea and Japan.
